## The context

Xubit builds custom management systems. I worked on the management panel of a health insurance provider: an internal system where every piece of data has to comply with the sector's regulations.

## What I did

- **Designed and built the panel's interface.** Every form validates its data thoroughly with Zod, so whatever enters the system meets the regulatory rules.
- **Led the front-end migration from Angular to React.** I rebuilt the whole application to make it easier to maintain and to let the team ship features faster.
- **Refactored part of the back-end.** I added new features and reduced errors in critical flows.

## What I took away

A regulated system doesn't forgive bad data. Keeping validation in one place, with typed schemas, made the rules explicit and easy to review.
