## The context

Logística Almagro needed a new company website that loaded fast, could be found on search engines and turned visits into enquiries.

## What I did

- **Built the site with Astro and Tailwind CSS.** I chose a lightweight architecture so pages load fast.
- **Added motion with intent.** The site has an entrance animation, scroll-driven animations, a reviews marquee and page transitions.
- **Integrated Resend into the contact form,** so enquiries reliably arrive by email.
- **Set up Google Tag Manager** to measure conversions.
- **Applied on-page SEO** to the content and structure, to improve organic ranking.
