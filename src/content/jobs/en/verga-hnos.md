## The context

Verga Hnos. is a moving and furniture storage company. UsáUsado, part of the same group, sells second-hand items. I worked on both products.

## Verga Hnos.

- **Built the company website with Next.js.** It includes server-side logic, such as newsletter sign-up through the Mailchimp API.
- **Designed a multi-step moving quote builder.** It replaced a form with more than 60 fields with a guided flow, with complex Zod validation and a custom interface. Each type of move and storage has its own form.
- **Built the rest of the site:** an entrance animation, modals to make some content easier to read, carousels and an image gallery.

## UsáUsado

- **Developed a batch upload system.** Before going live, every item is reviewed and approved by an expert.
